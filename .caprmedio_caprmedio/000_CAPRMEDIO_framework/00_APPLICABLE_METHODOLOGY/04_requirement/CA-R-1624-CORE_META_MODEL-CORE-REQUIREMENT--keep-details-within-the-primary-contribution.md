# Summary

Keep Details within the primary contribution

## Scope

supporting Details **in** an Atom's Main Content.

## Claim

an Atom's Details **means** supporting information that expands its primary contribution **without** changing its intent, applicability, **or** required outcome. Details **must not** contradict that contribution **or** introduce another independently replaceable Claim. for RMED, the primary contribution is the Claim read within its Scope; Details **must not** introduce an additional obligation **or** alter those applicability restrictions.
